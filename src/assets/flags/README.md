Country flags for the language switcher, from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT, © Panayiotis Lipiridis), 4×3 set.
`es.svg` is simplified (stripes only, no coat of arms) to keep it small at 20 px.
`cn.svg` is redrawn (plain polygons) to keep it small at 20 px.
`us.svg` is redrawn (plain stripes and star polygons) to keep it small at 20 px.
